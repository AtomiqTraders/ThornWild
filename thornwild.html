The Hollow King

North of Halden lies the Thornwild: pine forest, the drowned Mire to the west, the Frostpeaks to the north, the burning Ashlands to the east and a cold sea to the south.

Warden Morrow was the Lodge’s best tracker. He found the three Sigils the old world buried in the Hollow Crypt, the Frost Cavern and the Ember Forge, and tried to bind them. The binding hollowed him out. He became the Hollow King, sealed himself beneath the Old Ruins, and his hunger leaks into every creature in the wild.

Each Sigil still holds a power. Take them back from what guards them, and you can break the seal and end him. The Lodge has nothing left to give you but a workbench and a fire.
